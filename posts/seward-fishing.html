What First-Time Deep-Sea Fishing Feels Like | Seward, Alaska

June–July 2025

This was the first time I had ever fished, deep-sea fishing out of Seward, Alaska. I was sure I would catch nothing. I caught four rockfish. An unexpected gift.

Some people, once a fish is on, yank the rod up and crank the reel as hard as they can, and haul the fish in by force. I don’t. For me the pleasure is in playing it. The instant the rod bends, I lift a few times, take line, and — as expected — meet a lot of resistance. It feels like a large fish, so I stop forcing it, let the rod drop, and work it left and right. There are more rounds of pull. When it is more or less swimming on my terms, I take line fast, rest and play it again when I cannot, until it comes to the surface.

Once it was on the surface I lost interest. Underwater, the pull still had a kind of unknown. After it came up I did not want to look at that dark, ugly thing in the water. I asked a crewman to lift it. The fish rolled on the deck, dying hard. He stunned it with a club to the head. It looked like fun; I said I wanted to try. Then he tore the hook from its mouth and blood sprayed the deck. I felt sick and went back into the cabin. Knocking it out for play would have been fine; I did not want any of the blood on me. Good I had not stood closer.

That was how I took four rockfish without much trouble. The limit is two per person, so the last two went back. I changed bait for salmon. Salmon are harder. Ten-odd minutes, nothing. I moved to the bow. Still nothing. No one else was there; the swell was large; the boat pitched. I sat alone in the bow, squinting at mountains in cloud and a whale pod appearing and disappearing far off, and felt a little as if I had left the world. Twice a fish took the bait, and twice it ran when I went to the rod. No respect. I was not fishing seriously anyway.

After half an hour I still had no salmon, so I did get the empty-handed feeling. A crewman hooked a big gold-colored fish and let it go. People thought it a waste. I would have let it go too. However fine a fish looks, they more or less taste the same.

I have three stages of fishing. Before you start, it seems hard, you are a beginner, you will go home with nothing. When you have one on, playing it is interesting; once it is aboard, dealing with it is a bother. Catch enough and you cannot be bothered — fishing is only that, after all. Better to stand apart, take the wind, and look at the view.

第一次海钓是什么体验｜阿拉斯加Seward海钓

June–July 2025

这是我人生第一次钓鱼，在阿拉斯加的Seward海钓，本来以为肯定空军，结果钓到了四条石斑鱼，也算是意外之喜[偷笑R]

我看有些人喜欢上鱼之后就猛地往上拉杆并狂转线轴往回收鱼线，然后就这么暴力把鱼拽上来。但我则不然，我觉得，钓鱼最大的乐趣就在遛鱼。当看到鱼竿往下弯的一瞬间，先试探性地起几次竿并回收鱼线，不出所料地遇到很大阻力，看起来是条大鱼，于是手上不用力就让竿自然下垂，并左右摆动遛鱼，中间肯定又有几轮有趣的拉扯，等它差不多按着我的节奏游的时候，这时就突然急速往回收鱼线，然后感觉收不上来的时候又歇一会儿遛一遛鱼，直到鱼浮出水面。

其实鱼浮出水面之后，我就失去了兴趣，它在水下的时候我和它拉扯总归是有些未知的神秘感，但它浮上来后，我不想再看水里那黑黑的狰狞的东西。叫船员小哥来帮忙把鱼提上来后，鱼还在甲板上滚来滚去，垂死挣扎，小哥就用一根棒子敲它的脑袋给它敲晕，看起来很好玩，我跟他说我也想试一下，但紧接着他抓住鱼把鱼钩从它嘴里拽出来时溅出一地血，我感到有些恶心，就立即走回船舱里了，虽然敲着玩可以，但我不想沾上一点血腥，幸亏刚才没离的太近。

就这样，我轻轻松松地钓到了四条石斑鱼，因为每人限钓两条，所以后面两条都放生了。之后我就换了饵想钓三文鱼。但三文鱼可不好钓，等了十多分钟没见有鱼，我把钓位换到船头，仍不见鱼。船头没有人，风浪大，晃得厉害，我一个人坐在船头，眯着眼睛望着云雾缭绕的山峦，和远处忽隐若现的鲸鱼群，不禁有些许遗世独立之感。有两次，鱼上钩了，但我走过去拿住竿后又跑了，真是不给我面子，不过我反正也没有认真钓。

等了半个多小时还是没钓上三文鱼，算是体验到钓鱼佬空军的感觉了。不过船员小哥钓到了一条金色的大鱼，却把它放了，大家觉得有些可惜。不过如果是我，我也会放了的，毕竟再好看的鱼，吃起来也都差不多罢。

我钓鱼有三个阶段，没钓之前觉得鱼很难钓，自己又是新手，肯定空手而归；钓到的时候在拉扯遛鱼时觉得很有趣，钓上来了又觉得处理起来麻烦；钓多了就懒得钓了，感觉钓鱼这事也就这样吧，不如潇洒地遗世独立吹风赏景。
