Four Days in Miami on a Budget | My Second Solo Trip

2026

This was my second trip alone, and it felt like the hardest.

The first morning I flew from New York into FLL, which is far out. The Tri-Rail will take you downtown, but tickets on site are cash or card only, so I missed a train and waited an hour for the next. In the afternoon I took a car to South Beach. The beach deserves its name. Oddly, boats towed giant screens of ads across the water. Walk south along the sand and you reach South Pointe Park Pier, close enough to watch huge cruise ships pass, and a good sunset.

The second morning I joined an Everglades tour: the famous airboat, wild alligators, water birds. Next to it a small zoo of lizards and snakes; you can hold a baby alligator. Afternoon at Bayside Marketplace, where I bought a sunset cruise ticket. Then the free Mover, a driverless train that often runs through buildings, like Chongqing. Then the free old-fashioned trolley to the Wynwood Walls. Traffic was bad, and once there it was only so-so — not worth a special trip. Back to Bayside for the Millionaire Row Cruise. I did not know any of the famous names the guide listed (except Jackie Chan), but the sunset from the boat was good. Cuban food at night; the seafood was excellent.

Day three, Bill Baggs State Park. Do not go without a car. The bus leaves you at the gate with another half hour to walk. It is beautiful, though, and close to animals: lizards, great egrets, hawks. A lighthouse looks over the beach and the Miami skyline; it was closed when I was there. Afterward Brickell City Center, which felt like a mall at home.

Day four, metro to Vizcaya Museum & Gardens. Greek sculpture in the gardens with an old elegance; overall good. Afternoon in Little Havana. The huge murals are handsome. I bought a Cuban ice cream. It tasted like ordinary ice cream.

Note: Miami’s sights are scattered. Bring cash and a card. Bring sunscreen or you will burn on the beach. Don’t ask how I know.

迈阿密四天穷游｜人生第二次独自旅行

2026

这是我人生中第二次独自旅行，感觉是最艰难的一次。

第一天上午从纽约飞过来到FLL机场，这机场很偏，坐tri-rail可以到市中心，但现场买票只能现金或刷卡，导致我错过了一班，下一班等了一个小时[失望R]下午打车去了【South Beach】，这海滩确实是名不虚传，有趣的是海面上有船拖着巨型屏幕播放广告。沿着海滩一直往南走就可以到【South Pointe Park Pier】，可以近距离看巨大的游轮从你面前驶过，日落也很好看。

第二天上午我报了【Everglades】的tour，体验了著名的草上飞（游船），看到了野生鳄鱼和一些水鸟。然后旁边还有个动物园里面有蜥蜴和蛇等各种爬行动物，还可以上手摸小鳄鱼🐊下午先在【Bayside Marketplace】逛了一圈，买了个日落时分的游船票。之后就去市内坐免费的mover，这个挺有特色，无人驾驶的轻轨，经常穿楼而过，像在重庆。然后又坐免费的复古叮当车trolley去【Wynwood Wall】，但路上很堵，到了以后感觉一般，不用特意来一趟。然后又返回bayside去坐那个【Millionaire Row Cruise】，尽管导游介绍的那些名人我一个也不认识（除了成龙），但船上看日落还是不错的。晚上体验了古巴菜，海鲜挺好吃的。

第三天去了【Bill Baggs State Park】，没车真不建议来，因为坐公交到了门口以后还要走半个多小时才能到，但确实是个风景如画之地，而且能近距离看到很多动物，比如蜥蜴，大白鹭，鹰。还有个灯塔可以俯瞰海滩和迈阿密天际线，但我去的时候它没开。回来以后去【Brickell City Center】转了转，感觉神似国内的商场。

第四天坐地铁去【Vizcaya Museum & Gardens】，花园里的希腊雕塑有着古代的优雅，整体上不错。下午去了【Little Havana】，巨幅涂鸦很好看，买了个古巴雪糕，感觉跟普通的没啥区别。

注：迈阿密的景点比较分散。带现金和银行卡。带防晒霜，不然在海滩肯定得晒伤，别问我怎么知道的[捂脸R]
