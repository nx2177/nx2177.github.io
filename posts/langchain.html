Six Rounds with LangChain. Then This.

January 2026

2025.11.15
HR reached out by email and set an interview for 11.19, then moved it to 11.24 for their own reasons.

2025.11.24
Round one was a standard HR screen, half an hour. They would share my résumé with the whole team and decide from feedback whether to send a take-home.

2025.12.2
The take-home arrived at eight in the morning. I turned it in at eleven that night. Three people on the team reviewed it; call that round two. A LangSmith-related project.

2025.12.10
HR wrote that the assignment had gotten positive feedback, and booked system design and a project review.

2025.12.18
Round three, system design, one hour. I did not know it well. Round four, project review, half an hour, walking through the take-home. Two hours later, an email to meet the VP the next day.

2025.12.19
Round five with the VP of Engineering, half an hour, ordinary behavioral questions: projects I had done, the largest challenge. I thought this was the last round, and waited.

2025.12.22
HR emailed that there was still one more, after the holidays, the week of January 5.

2026.1.5
They scheduled round six and asked when I was free the next day.

2026.1.6
Round six with a founding engineer, half an hour. They went through the résumé projects more closely than the VP had.

2026.1.20
I followed up by email. An HR text. No.

I was eating lunch in the Natural History Museum in Washington, D.C. I had a strawberry croissant and a hot chocolate. I opened the message, saw it, and drank another sip. Too sweet. A huge shark hung in the hall. I leaned back and looked at it. I wondered what would happen if it came alive. It would probably eat me as a snack. And then?

I was not surprised, or angry, or sad. I felt nothing. I only looked at the shark. Its black eye was bright, as if it were looking at me too. The hot chocolate went down my throat with a sandy sound. Children laughed in a clutter of noise. Sunlight came through the glass in the ceiling.

Let it be.

LangChain面了六轮，结果

January 2026

2025.11.15
HR主动reach out by email，并约面试时间11.19，但后面因为HR的原因改期到11.24

2025.11.24
第一轮就是正常HR screening，半个小时，最后说会把我简历share给整个team，看team的反馈决定是否给我发take home

2025.12.2
早上八点发了take home assignment, 当天晚上11点我就交了，之后就是三个团队成员审核，这个就算第二轮吧。是一个跟LangSmith相关的项目

2025.12.10
HR联系我说作业收到了positive feedback ，约下一轮system design和project review

2025.12.18
第三轮system design是一个小时，不太会。第四轮project review是半个小时，就是过一下之前那个作业。面完后过了两个小时，收到邮件约明天面VP

2025.12.19
第五轮跟VP of Engineer聊，半个小时，就正常的BQ面，问做过的项目和最大挑战是什么。我本来以为这一轮是终面，就等结果

2025.12.22
HR发邮件说还有一轮，由于放假说1.5那周再面

2026.1.5
约第六轮，问我明天什么时候有空

2026.1.6
第六轮跟founding engineer聊，半个小时，简历项目问的比VP更仔细一些

2026.1.20
发邮件follow up。收到HR短信。🈚️了。

当时，我在华盛顿DC的自然历史博物馆里吃午饭，我点了一个草莓味可颂和一杯热巧克力，打开message，看到那条信息，我又喝了一口热巧克力，太甜了。大厅悬挂着一条巨大的鲨鱼，我靠在椅背上，静静地和它对视着，我忽然想，如果它活过来会怎样？大概会把我当点心，不过那又如何。

我并不惊讶，也不愤怒，也不悲伤，我没有任何感觉，只是看着那条鲨鱼，它的黑眼珠很亮，仿佛也在看着我。热巧克力在我喉咙里沙沙地滑过。一群小孩子在嘈杂地嬉笑着。阳光从天花板的玻璃上照射下来。

就这样罢。
