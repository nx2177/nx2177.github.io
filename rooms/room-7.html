Room VII

Yellowstone

The yellow stone on the desk is the door. Photographs from the park will hang here as they are printed — steam, bison, and that long evening light.

The exhibition is being prepared

Until the prints arrive, this room holds the idea of the place: earth that breathes, water that should not be blue and yet is, light that turns everything the color of the stone on the desk.
